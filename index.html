Published research

◎ Evidence standards

How we read the market

Separating promising signals from hype, so trends are actionable rather than just exciting screenshots.

01

Only comparable measurements

Completed daily-average concurrent player figures are graphed together only within a compatible series from one tracker. Instant counts, sampled peaks, rolling 24h averages and weekly averages are labeled separately.

02

Look for sustained breakouts

A game crossing 5K or 20K average CCU is noteworthy, but multiple days of sustained activity are stronger evidence than a single spike. Early tracking windows can exaggerate growth.

03

Separate correlation from cause

Updates, content creators and seasonal events may coincide with growth. Unless evidence supports it, we don't claim that one specific change caused the increase.

04

Translate numbers into decisions

The ongoing research tracks mechanics, longevity, competition, build feasibility for a smaller developer, and seasonal timing. A high-confidence game concept or launch window has not yet been established.

Independent research sources

Reported measurements and analyses in the supplied archive cite public trackers including RoVitals ↗, BloxRadar ↗, RoStats ↗, Statroblox ↗ and RBLX Maven ↗. Open any game to see the link supporting its plotted history. The site does not query those services live.
